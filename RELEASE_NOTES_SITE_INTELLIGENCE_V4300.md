# Site Intelligence v4.16.0 — Astronomical Observation Environment

Extends Earth Observation → Orbital Earth → Lunar & Planetary Intelligence into source-aware deep-sky observation while preserving the v4 six-area / 35-route architecture.

## Added
- Six deep-sky target presets with explicit equatorial J2000 field centers.
- Seven initial multi-wavelength survey families spanning optical, near/mid infrared, ultraviolet, radio, soft X-ray, and microwave/submillimeter context.
- NASA/IPAC IRSA Finder Chart handoffs for DSS, 2MASS, and WISE.
- NASA/GSFC HEASARC SkyView handoffs for GALEX, NVSS, ROSAT, and Planck survey families.
- Field-of-view state, wavelength/color-semantics disclosure, archive/source provenance, and evidence manifests.
- Direct and WordPress-iframe Astronomical Observation panel inside the existing Earth route.

## Scientific boundary
The local star field is an orientation surface, not telescope or survey pixels. Archive products may have different epochs, projections, resolutions, calibration pipelines, and display mappings. No live-telescope, real-time acquisition, natural-color, or universal-coverage claim is made.
