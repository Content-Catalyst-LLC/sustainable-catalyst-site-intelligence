# Site Intelligence v4.26.0 — Global Human Settlements, Urbanization & Built Environment Intelligence

## Purpose
Extend Earth Observation into built-up morphology, modeled population distribution, nighttime radiance and harmonized urbanization statistics while preserving provenance and non-inference boundaries.

## Registered source families
1. JRC Global Human Settlement Layer (GHSL) — built-up surface/volume, population grids and settlement model.
2. WorldPop Global2 API — modeled population and age/sex estimates.
3. NASA VIIRS Black Marble — nighttime-radiance and derived nighttime-lights products.
4. World Bank Indicators API — harmonized country-level urbanization statistics.

## Truth boundary
**HUMAN SETTLEMENT EVIDENCE · NOT A CENSUS, PROPERTY, ZONING OR INFRASTRUCTURE-SERVICE DETERMINATION**

Built-up grids are not parcel footprints; modeled epochs are not direct observations for every year; population estimates are not census headcounts; nighttime lights are not automatically electricity service, economic output or population; urban indicators are not settlement/zoning boundaries; and zero returned records do not establish an uninhabited area.

## Architecture
Six primary areas and all 35 public routes remain unchanged. Human Settlements is deferred from the existing Agriculture environment inside Earth Observation.
