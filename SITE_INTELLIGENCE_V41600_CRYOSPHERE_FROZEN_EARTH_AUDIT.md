## Sources

## Preserved boundaries
- near-real-time != final
- missing data != no ice or snow
- inventory geometry != current position
- glacier outline != mass balance
- modeled field != observation
- frozen-ground map != verified local permafrost
- pixel/classification != local travel or safety determination
- anomaly != hazard declaration or causal attribution
