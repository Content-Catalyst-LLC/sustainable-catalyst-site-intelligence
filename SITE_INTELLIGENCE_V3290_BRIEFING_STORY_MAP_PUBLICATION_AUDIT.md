## Required boundaries
- No automatic publication or public write.
- Human editorial review remains mandatory.
- Methodology and limitations remain visible.
- Visual blocks require accessible descriptions for a clean accessibility result.
- Story order, spatial proximity, chart alignment, and temporal adjacency do not establish causation.
- Correction records preserve prior versions.
- Frozen-manifest hashes are integrity/change fingerprints, not truth or authority certifications.
