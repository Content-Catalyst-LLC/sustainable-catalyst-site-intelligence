# v4.16.0 Ocean Events, Hazards & Ecosystem Change Audit

## Scope
- Marine heatwave / thermal anomaly
- Coral bleaching heat stress
- Harmful algal bloom
- Hypoxia / low dissolved oxygen
- Sea-ice anomaly
- Extreme wave conditions
- Storm-driven ocean impact
- Ecosystem change signal

## Source families
NOAA Coral Reef Watch; NOAA CoastWatch/OceanWatch; Copernicus Marine; NOAA NCCOS.

## Assurance boundaries
The platform never promotes a threshold, forecast, model field, advisory, or sparse observation into an automatic hazard declaration. Source-issued classifications remain source-attributed. No automatic action is authorized. Environmental stress is not represented as observed ecosystem impact unless that impact is separately evidenced.

## Interface
The deferred Ocean Events panel is loaded from the Ocean Missions environment and is explicitly labeled `ORIENTATION FIELD · NOT AN OFFICIAL WARNING`.
