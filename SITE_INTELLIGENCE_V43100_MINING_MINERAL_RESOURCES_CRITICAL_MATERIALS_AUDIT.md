# Site Intelligence v4.31.0 Mining / Mineral Resources / Critical Materials Audit

## Scope
Global mining and mineral-resource orientation inside the existing Earth Observation chain.

## Source roles
- OpenStreetMap: community-mapped mine/quarry/shaft/adit/tailings geometry.
- USGS USMIN: authoritative U.S. deposit/resource records.
- USGS MCS 2026: public-domain annual commodity production/reserve statistics.
- IEA Critical Minerals Data Explorer: CC BY 4.0 scenario-based demand/supply projections.

## Required truth boundaries
Mapped feature ≠ operating mine. Resource ≠ certified reserve. National production ≠ mine output. Scenario ≠ observation or guaranteed forecast. Scenario gap ≠ shortage. Critical-mineral designation ≠ investment recommendation or national-security finding. Zero records ≠ absence of mineral resources.
