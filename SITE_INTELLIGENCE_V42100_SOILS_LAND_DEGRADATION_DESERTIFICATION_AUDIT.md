# v4.21.0 Soils, Land Degradation & Desertification Audit

## Evidence classes

- modelled-soil-property-map
- official-soil-survey-mapunit
- official-soil-survey-tabular-record
- satellite-soil-moisture-retrieval
- model-assimilated-soil-moisture
- country-reported-land-degradation-indicator
- unccd-default-estimate

## Indicator classes

Soil organic carbon; clay, sand and silt fractions; bulk density; cation exchange capacity; pH; soil taxonomic class; hydrologic soil group; surface and root-zone soil moisture; degraded-land proportion; land-cover change; land productivity; and soil-organic-carbon change.

## Guard assertions

- Predictive soil maps are not ground truth.
- Survey map units are not parcel/site-specific determinations.
- Satellite retrievals are not in-situ probes.
- Model-assimilated root-zone moisture is not a direct observation.
- Partial country reporting is not a comprehensive global assessment.
- Land-degradation indicators are not automatic causal attribution.
- Soil carbon evidence is not carbon-credit verification.
- No automatic regulatory, engineering, agricultural-management or certification action is authorized.
