# v4.1.0 Institutional Workspaces and Review Governance Audit

- Existing v2.22 institutional workspace operations are retained as the persistence foundation.
- v3.30 adds portable preview/export/import contracts instead of a second workspace database.
- Preparation, reviewer approval, and publisher approval are distinct roles.
- Public-safe portable packages exclude credentials and personal contact fields.
- Audit previews are hash chained but do not claim to be a complete historical system of record.
- Public accounts and paid multi-tenant infrastructure are not required.
- Automatic approval, import, publication, and remote delivery remain disabled.
