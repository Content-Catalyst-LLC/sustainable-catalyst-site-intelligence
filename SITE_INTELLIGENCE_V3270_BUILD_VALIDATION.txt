Research integration behaviors
- Normalizes selected countries, indicators, events, sources, records, and saved-view context into a public-safe research context.
- Retains source snapshots with publisher, source id, safe HTTP(S) source URL, observation date, retrieval date, and deterministic SHA-256 change fingerprints.
- Produces evidence manifests with truth state, limitations, and record-level provenance.
- Produces de-duplicated citation exports without claiming that a citation establishes authority or truth.
- Builds claim/evidence maps with supports, contradicts, qualifies, and contextualizes relationships without automatically resolving contested claims.
- Prepares Knowledge Library discovery queries while explicitly distinguishing a query plan from verified indexed matches.
- Prepares preview-only handoff packets for Research Librarian, Knowledge Library, Workbench, and Decision Studio.
- Handoff packets require explicit human confirmation; delivery_attempted=false, delivery_verified=false, publication_allowed=false.
- Rejects sensitive key names and strips unsafe non-HTTP(S) source URLs from public handoff context.
