# v4.25.0 Agriculture, Crops & Food-System Conditions Audit

## Source registry
- FAOSTAT — global official food and agriculture statistics.
- USDA NASS Quick Stats — official U.S. agricultural estimates from surveys and Census of Agriculture.
- USDA Crop-CASMA / VegScape — SMAP/MODIS-derived crop vegetation and soil-moisture condition context.
- GEOGLAM Crop Monitor — multi-source consensus crop-condition assessments.

## Non-inference controls
- Official statistics are not field observations or exact counts.
- EO crop condition is not yield measurement.
- GEOGLAM condition classes remain source-issued assessments, not platform forecasts.
- Food-balance statistics are not household or national food-security determinations.
- Empty results do not prove crop absence or absence of stress.
- Threshold previews do not issue crop-loss, market or food-security decisions.
