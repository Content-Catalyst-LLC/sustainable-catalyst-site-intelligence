# Site Intelligence v4.51.0 — Spatial Research Object & Cross-Product Handoffs

v4.51.0 packages a complete Site Intelligence investigation into one content-addressed spatial research object. The object can preserve canonical v4.46 spatial evidence, v4.47 spatiotemporal results, v4.48 relationship graphs, v4.49 live geospatial events, and v4.50 source-federation context without mutating any source record.

Seven provider-neutral handoff adapters are included: Workspace, Knowledge Library, Research Librarian, Research Lab, Workbench, Decision Studio, and Platform Core. Handoff packets are previews only: Site Intelligence does not deliver, publish, remotely write, or claim downstream import success.

The release also adds a multi-target reproducible spatial research package, a manifest contract, digest validation, a sensitive-field rejection boundary, standalone navigation/bootstrap discovery, and a new first-class modular capability family.
