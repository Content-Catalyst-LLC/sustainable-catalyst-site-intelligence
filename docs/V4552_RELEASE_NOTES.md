# Site Intelligence v4.55.2 — Advanced Domain Intelligence Workspace Expansion

v4.55.2 converts six previously shallow domain workspaces into first-class analytical research surfaces: Dossiers, Economics, International Law, Science, Humanitarian, and Resources.

The release adds 21 modular `/public/domain-intelligence` routes, six domain-specific analytical engines, deterministic research packets, a six-domain parity gate, and an Advanced Research panel inside each standalone workspace. The browser analyzes the official records already loaded by the existing workspace controller rather than creating a separate simulated dataset.

Boundaries remain explicit: missing records are not fabricated, units are not silently normalized, and the system does not automatically infer causality, legal conclusions, culpability, investment advice, sanctions status, or security risk.
