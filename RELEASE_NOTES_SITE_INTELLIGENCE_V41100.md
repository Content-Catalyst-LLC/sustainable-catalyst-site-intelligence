# Site Intelligence v4.16.0 — Ocean Events, Hazards & Ecosystem Change

Extends the ocean observation continuum with source-attributed event, anomaly, hazard-context, and ecosystem-change evidence. Registered source families: NOAA Coral Reef Watch, NOAA CoastWatch/OceanWatch, Copernicus Marine, and NOAA NCCOS.

The release preserves strict evidence boundaries: a threshold crossing is not a hazard declaration; forecasts are not observations; model analyses are not in-situ measurements; coral heat stress does not by itself prove bleaching or mortality; HAB signals do not by themselves prove toxin exposure or impact; low oxygen at one sample does not prove a persistent regional dead zone; source advisories are not reissued as Sustainable Catalyst warnings; and missing records do not prove safe conditions.

All public routes remain within the existing Earth Observation workspace. The six-area / 35-route v4 architecture is unchanged.
