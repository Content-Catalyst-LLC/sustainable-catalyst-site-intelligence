# Site Intelligence v4.16.0 — Global Cryosphere Intelligence & Frozen-Earth Conditions

Adds a provenance-first cryosphere environment inside Earth Observation covering sea ice, snow, glaciers, ice sheets and frozen-ground evidence. The six-primary-area / 35-route public architecture is preserved.

Truth boundary: **CRYOSPHERE EVIDENCE · NOT A LOCAL SAFETY OR HAZARD DETERMINATION**. Near-real-time products remain distinct from final products; missing data is not treated as no ice/snow; inventory geometry is not treated as current position; model analysis is not silently relabeled as observation; mapped frozen ground does not prove local permafrost; and the platform does not issue travel, hazard or safety determinations.
