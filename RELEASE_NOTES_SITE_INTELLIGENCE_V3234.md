# Site Intelligence v3.23.6.1

## Analytical Workspace Completion

This release completes the five core public analytical workflows: Global Conditions, Country Intelligence, Compare, Spatial Evidence, and Earth Observation. Each workflow now has one explicit contract for inputs, stages, outputs, deep links, source truth, exports, and initial, ready, empty, degraded, and unavailable states.

### Boundaries

Missing values are not silently imputed. Cached, historical, contextual, and demonstration records cannot be presented as live. Spatial proximity and temporal sequence do not establish causation. Imagery outages do not remove vector geography or provenance.
