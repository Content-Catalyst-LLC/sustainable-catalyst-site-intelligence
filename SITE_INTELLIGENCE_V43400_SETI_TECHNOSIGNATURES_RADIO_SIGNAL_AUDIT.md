# Site Intelligence v4.34.0 — SETI / Technosignature Evidence Audit

## Governing boundary

**TECHNOSIGNATURE SEARCH EVIDENCE · NOT CONFIRMATION OF EXTRATERRESTRIAL INTELLIGENCE**

## Registered source roles

### Breakthrough Listen Open Data Archive
Official public archive: `https://seti.berkeley.edu/opendata`

The archive exposes search/filter behavior across targets, telescopes, file/data types, sky position, time and center frequency and references backend API documentation. Public data products can be very large and require specialist analysis software. Site Intelligence treats archive results as observation/data-product evidence only.

### Breakthrough Listen 1327-Star Event Tables
Official data release: `https://seti.berkeley.edu/listen2019/opendata.html`

Published event-group tables expose source target, drift-rate summaries, center frequency, bandwidth, individual frequencies/drift rates, file IDs and SNR. Site Intelligence preserves these as signal-processing event records. An event group is not a technosignature confirmation.

### SETI Institute Technosearch
Official public archive: `https://technosearch.seti.org/`

Technosearch records published radio, optical and archival SETI searches to document where and how searches have been conducted. Site Intelligence treats search coverage as methodology/history evidence. A non-detection does not establish absence of extraterrestrial technology.

### NASA Exoplanet Archive TAP
Official TAP documentation: `https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/TAP/usingTAP.html`

The TAP service supports programmatic planetary-system queries, including coordinates and confirmed-planet context. Site Intelligence uses these records only to orient SETI targets. Exoplanet presence, habitability context or stellar metadata is not technosignature evidence.

## Candidate lifecycle boundary

Breakthrough Listen publications provide concrete examples of why source labels must remain bounded. BLC1 was investigated as a signal of interest and ultimately attributed to human interference. A separate machine-learning search identified signals of interest that were not re-detected on follow-up and therefore did not satisfy bona fide technosignature-candidate criteria.

The v4.34 contract therefore fixes these inferences to false:

- signal event equals technosignature
- candidate equals confirmed extraterrestrial intelligence
- non-detection equals absence of technology
- radio-frequency interference excluded by the platform
- pipeline score equals scientific confirmation
- target context equals technosignature evidence
- one observation authorizes announcement
- automatic action authorized

## Announcement / verification boundary

Site Intelligence does not act as a SETI detection authority. Candidate confirmation and public announcement require scientific review and independent verification outside the platform. The platform can preserve evidence, provenance, source dispositions and follow-up records; it cannot convert them into a discovery claim.
