# Site Intelligence v4.23.0 — Global Biodiversity, Species Distribution & Conservation Intelligence

## Purpose
Extend Earth Observation from climate and land-system context into biodiversity occurrence, marine and avian observations, and conservation-governance evidence without adding a new top-level route family.

## Source registry
1. **GBIF Occurrence API** — global biodiversity occurrence search and source-dataset context.
2. **OBIS** — global marine biodiversity occurrence/event data with underlying dataset licenses and QC.
3. **eBird Public Data & APIs** — bird observations, checklists and broader downloadable data products.
4. **USFWS ECOS** — U.S. ESA species, recovery-document and critical-habitat data services.

## Truth boundary
**BIODIVERSITY EVIDENCE · NOT A POPULATION CENSUS, ABSENCE FINDING OR LEGAL DETERMINATION**

Occurrence records are not population estimates; zero records are not species absence; observations are not automatic current-occupancy or breeding confirmation; USFWS ESA status is U.S.-specific rather than a global conservation category; and critical-habitat overlap is not a project-effects, consultation, take or compliance determination.

## Architecture
Six primary areas and 35 public routes remain unchanged. Biodiversity is a deferred Earth Observation environment after Climate.
