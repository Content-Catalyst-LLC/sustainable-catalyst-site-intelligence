## Truth boundaries
- No fabricated live spacecraft position, ephemeris, ground track, or instantaneous sensor swath.
- Orbit rings are illustrative interface orientation graphics.
- Perspective altitude controls visual navigation and is not claimed as solved spacecraft camera geometry.
- Historical/composited imagery remains date- and source-labeled.
