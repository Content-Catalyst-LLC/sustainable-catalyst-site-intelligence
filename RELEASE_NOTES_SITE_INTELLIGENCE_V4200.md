# Site Intelligence v4.14.0 — Lunar & Planetary Intelligence

v4.14.0 extends Orbital Earth into source-aware Moon and Mars destinations without adding a new top-level route. It registers NASA Solar System Treks and USGS Astrogeology product sources, exposes mission/instrument/product provenance, body-specific coordinates, coverage and quantitative-use limitations, and reproducible planetary evidence manifests.

The local planetary body graphic is orientation-only. It is never represented as mission imagery. Official imagery opens through registered NASA/USGS source services until a verified raster endpoint is explicitly connected.
