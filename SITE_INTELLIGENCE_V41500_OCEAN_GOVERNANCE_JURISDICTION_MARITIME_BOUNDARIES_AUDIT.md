# Site Intelligence v4.15.0 — Ocean Governance Audit

## Source registry

1. NOAA Office of Coast Survey — U.S. Maritime Limits & Boundaries.
2. Marine Regions / Flanders Marine Institute (VLIZ) — global maritime-boundary WMS/WFS and downloadable datasets.
3. FAO Major Fishing Areas — statistical fishing-area framework.
4. FAO Regional Fishery Bodies — institutional and management-area context.

## Legal and evidentiary controls

- Geometry is orientation/evidence, not a Sustainable Catalyst legal opinion.
- Statistical fishing areas are not silently promoted to jurisdiction.
- Regional Fishery Body coverage is not sovereignty.
- Spatial overlap is not a legal-conflict determination.
- No fishing authorization, enforcement action, treaty entitlement, navigation instruction, or disputed-boundary resolution is inferred.
- Source version, method, dispute status, known issues, and source authority remain visible when supplied.
